How to build a weekly posture habit that actually sticks

Published 2026-06-17 · Recover

Weekly habits are easier to keep when they are attached to a clear trigger and a small win. Posture work fits best when it feels like maintenance, not a project.

Use a weekly target

Pick one or two posture drills and do them on the same days each week. That removes decision fatigue and keeps the habit from growing legs.

Keep the action tiny

  • Wall angels.
  • Chest opener against a doorway.
  • Shoulder blade squeezes.

Track the repeat

Seeing a weekly progress bar is enough to make the habit feel real. That is often better than trying to “feel motivated” every time.
