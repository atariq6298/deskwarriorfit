Science

Evidence, not hype.

The routines are based on small, repeatable actions that match how habits actually stick: low friction, visible progress, and enough structure to remove decision fatigue.

Why the system is split into Move, Recover, Fuel

Move

Frequent brief movement helps interrupt long sedentary blocks and reduces the feeling of stiffness that builds over a workday.

Recover

Recovery routines help the body downshift after repeated screen exposure and fixed posture.

Fuel

Hydration and meal timing support energy stability better than waiting for motivation to show up.

How challenges are selected

Low effort

If it takes too long, it won’t happen during a workday. Most prompts are built to fit between meetings.

Repeatable

Every routine can be done again tomorrow without special equipment or setup.

Visible progress

Weekly targets and streaks turn invisible effort into something you can actually see and keep up.
