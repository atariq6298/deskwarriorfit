Privacy

Your data stays focused on your routine.

We only use account and progress data to power your dashboard, authentication, and the routines you choose to track.

What we collect

  • Email address for account sign-in.
  • Progress events you save in the app.
  • Basic usage data needed to keep the site working.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run ads based on your data.
  • We do not share your progress publicly unless you choose to.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, use the site contact links or open an issue in the repository.
