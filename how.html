How it works

Small prompts. Repeated well.

DeskWarriorFit keeps the day simple: daily routines, weekly focus, and a dashboard that tells you exactly what to do next.

1. Pick your flow

MOVE
Daily motion prompts

Short breaks like stretch reminders and posture resets.

RECOVER
Screen recovery

Eye breaks, wind-down cues, and posture support.

FUEL
Hydration and food timing

Water reminders and steady-energy habits throughout the workday.

2. Check off what you complete

Routines

Water and stretching can repeat each day and show progress in the dashboard.

Weekly goals

Posture and strength work can happen once or twice each week without pressure.

Day plan

See the week at a glance and open the daily challenge plan when you need it.
